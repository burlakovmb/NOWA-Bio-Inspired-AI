## Meaning and observability of the metrics

Senior's source-only reading of `procurement_fitness.py:351–414` establishes that both Stage 1b metrics use accepted orders; waits and rejected orders are excluded. `decision_consistency` groups by `product_id`, computes each repeated product's largest supplier share and averages those shares without product-volume weighting. It returns `0.5` for fewer than four accepted orders or no repeated product. This measures supplier concentration within each product over a quarter; the implementation does not group by situation or time, despite the docstring's broader wording.

Diversification uses orders for products with at least two reachable suppliers at quarter end: `0.6×(1−largest_supplier_share)+0.4×min(1,supplier_count/3)`. Fewer than two accepted orders gives `0.5`; no contestable products gives `1.0`. Per-product loyalty and overall supplier spread can coexist when different products have different preferred suppliers. Their formulas alone establish neither incompatible thresholds nor an agent/core defect.
